01The platform

Take it apart. It still adds up.

The KPI strip, the sales chart and the trial balance read the same double-entry ledger. Two screens can never show two different numbers for the same day.

  • Each company in its own workspace, at its own address like rahman-traders.omnivo.app.
  • Roles and permissions decide which card each person sees.
02Works offline

The outbox keeps the counter open.

Load-shedding stops the router, not the shop. Sales, orders and new customers are saved in a sealed copy on the device and sent in order when the line is back.

  • 7 days of new work without a sync.
  • A change waits for the one it needs: the order goes after its new customer.
03Accounts

The books close themselves.

Invoices, receipts, bills and POS shifts post their own journals. Trial balance, profit and loss, balance sheet, aging and the VAT report are ready whenever you open them.

  • Lakh and crore grouping, kept to the paisa.
  • VAT by its Mushak name, zero-rated and exempt kept apart.
04POS counter

The receipt prints, online or not.

Scan, take cash or bKash, print the 80 mm receipt. Shifts open and close with a blind count, and the X and Z reports come off the same printer.

  • Returns and exchanges inside the register's own window.
  • Over and short posted to the account you choose.
05Online shop

Your own shop, in your colours.

Every workspace gets a shop at its own address. Orders arrive as sales orders, parcels book with Steadfast at Packed, and the rider's cash returns as a courier payout matched to the bank.

  • Cash on delivery or Pay online through SSLCommerz.
  • Tracking by SMS link, no app for the shopper.
06Put it together

Your company, on one screen.

Pick the address your team signs in at. The chart of accounts, VAT rates and the July to June year come set up for Bangladesh.
